Community Research — Roots Music Festival (Boulder)

This page documents the needs assessment and planning used to shape the Roots Boulder community hub. It’s intentionally not in the main nav, but is linked from the footer for context and grading.

Primary Needs

  • Accessible, mobile-first schedule and lineup.
  • Centralized info: maps, ADA routes, shuttles, allowed items, tickets.
  • Volunteer and vendor coordination with clear requirements and forms.

Current Gaps

  • Info scattered across social posts—no one mobile hub.
  • Accessibility & transit details often buried or inconsistent.
  • Vendor info not standardized for quick comparison.

Target Users

  • Attendees (local & travelers)
  • Artists/Managers
  • Vendors
  • Volunteers
  • Accessibility-focused visitors

Resource Statement

“My community resource site will help Roots Music Festival by centralizing schedule, maps/accessibility, and participation workflows so that members can quickly plan their day, navigate the grounds, and engage with tickets, volunteer, and vendor signups.”

Planned Features

  • Interactive schedule with filters (day/stage) and “on now”.
  • Festival map with ADA overlays, shuttle stops, hydration/first aid.
  • Ticket, volunteer, and vendor flows with clear requirements.
  • Weather and live updates banner during festival days.

Success Metrics

  • 80%+ task success within 60 seconds.
  • Mobile bounce rate under 35% during the event.
  • Positive accessibility feedback (routes, viewing areas, shuttles).
