Who I Am

I’m a student designer who bridges strategy, copy, and code. I like building clear content architecture first, then layering visuals and micro‑interactions. Interests include brand systems, packaging, and logos.

Meaningful alt text, descriptive links, and keyboard support are non‑negotiable.

Iterative Process

Sketch → structure → prototype → feedback → refine. Design is a conversation.
