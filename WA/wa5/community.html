Community Research — Roots Music Festival (Boulder)

This page documents the needs assessment and planning used to shape the Roots Boulder community hub. It’s intentionally not in the main nav to keep the public site streamlined, but is linked from the footer for context and grading.

Community Overview

The Roots Music Festival in Boulder celebrates folk, bluegrass, and Americana, connecting artists, audiences, vendors, and volunteers. The digital hub aims to centralize live information and participation flows.

Primary Needs

  • Accessible, mobile-first schedule and lineup.
  • Centralized info: maps, ADA routes, shuttles, allowed items, tickets.
  • Volunteer and vendor coordination with clear requirements and forms.

Current Gaps

  • Info scattered across social posts—no one mobile hub.
  • Accessibility & transit details often buried or inconsistent.
  • Vendor info not standardized for quick comparison.

Target Users

  • Attendees (local & travelers)
  • Artists/Managers
  • Vendors
  • Volunteers
  • Accessibility-focused visitors

Resource Statement

“My community resource site will help Roots Music Festival by centralizing schedule, maps/accessibility, and participation so that members can quickly plan their day, navigate the grounds, and engage with tickets, volunteer, and vendor signups.”

Planned Features

  • Interactive schedule with filters (day/stage) and “on now”.
  • Festival map with ADA overlays, shuttle stops, hydration/first aid.
  • Ticket, volunteer, and vendor flows with clear requirements.
  • Weather and live updates banner during festival days.
